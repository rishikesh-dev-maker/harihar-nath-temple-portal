Sacred Confluence

Welcome to Baba Hariharnath Temple

Nestled in the sacred landscape of Sonepur, where the Ganga and Gandak rivers meet, this ancient temple stands as a testament to centuries of devotion and spiritual harmony.

Hari + Har Confluence

The divine union of Lord Vishnu (Hari) and Lord Shiva (Har) — an unparalleled confluence of Vaishnavism and Shaivism in one sanctum sanctorum.

Sacred Sangam

Positioned at the holy confluence of the sacred Ganga and Gandak rivers, drawing millions of pilgrims from all over India during Kartik Purnima.

Centuries of Faith

With ancient origins tracing to the Ramayana era and Gajendra Moksha legend, the temple stands as a timeless beacon of Dharma and peace.

Living Tradition

A thriving pilgrimage center where daily Vedic rituals, abhisheks, and evening aartis are conducted with pure Vedic rites and devotion.

Heritage

The Sacred Timeline

Centuries of devotion, preserved in stone, scripture, and sacred ritual

Puranic

Gajendra Moksha & Ram-Vishwamitra

Traditional scriptures record Lord Vishnu's deliverance of Gajendra and Lord Rama's visit accompanied by Sage Vishwamitra on their way to Janakpur.

~1500

Traditional Narratives

The Harihar Kshetra established itself as a paramount pilgrimage center for northern India, uniting Shiva and Vishnu devotees.

Pre-1757

Early Temple Sanctum

The consecrated stone shrine and Shivalinga of Hariharnath was fortified by regional chieftains and devotees.

1757+

Temple Complex Renovation

Raja Ram Narain and subsequent patrons reconstructed the towering Shikhara, Mandapa, and ghats at the riverfront.

Present

Modern Harihar Nath Temple Digital Era

Today, the temple stands as a modern pilgrimage hub with digital darshan booking, live timings, and streamlined facilities.

Architecture

Sacred Architecture

The temple's architecture reflects classical North Indian Nagara elements with sacred geometric alignments.

Every pillar, arch, sanctum door, and kalasha is aligned according to traditional Vastu and temple architecture canons. The inner sanctum houses the sacred Harihar Shivalinga, crowned by a high Shikhara that overlooks the holy river basin.

Sacred Mandapa

Spacious gathering hall for devotional kirtan, sankalp rituals, and prayer assemblies.

Garbhagriha

The sanctum sanctorum illuminated by pure brass deepaks where the sacred deity receives daily abhishekam.

Festivals

Sacred Celebrations

Rejoice in the grand celebrations of Harihar Kshetra

Kartik Purnima

The holiest confluence bathing day, celebrated with 108 deepak mahapuja and millions of pilgrims.

Sonepur Mela

Asia's historic cultural gathering rooted in temple tradition, spiritual discourse, and fairs.

Shravan Mahotsav

Holy month of Somwar vrat, Kanwar pilgrimages, and continuous sacred Ganga jal offering.

Maha Shivratri

The great sacred night of Shiva, observed with four-pahar abhisheka and all-night chanting.
